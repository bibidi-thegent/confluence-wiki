Start here

Confluence is a friends' Minecraft server: Java Edition survival with Confluence's classes, Breaches and professions on top. Pick an Expertise and one of its Disciplines, fight through the Breaches and the Manor with a party, level ten professions, build a town and trade at the market.

Bibidi runs it. To reach him, post in the Discord or message him there: questions, bugs and whitelist requests all go there. Playing needs a whitelist entry and the server's address, which he sends you himself; neither is on this site.

What each page holds

  • Join: what to install, the steps to your first login, the keys you need on day one, and what to do when it does not work.
  • Classes and specs: your bar, targeting, keys and the death penalty; Talents and Mastery; fights; then a page for each Discipline with its abilities and how to play it.
  • The world: homes, lifts, towns and the Manor; teleport requests; locks and benches; the market; waypoints; the Quarry and its hub; the hub's gates; Echoes, the Chronicle, incursions and scenarios; Confluence Caches; Deeds; packs; and Mob Nets.
  • Professions: the rules, then a page for each of the ten professions.
  • Commands: every command a player can use, in the groups /cf help shows in game.
  • Leaderboards: the top places on each board of the hub's Records, as of the last publish.
  • What's new: every update, newest first, as the Discord update threads posted it.

The pages are made from the same Player Guide and update notes the Discord gets, and they are refreshed after each update. In game, /cf help lists the commands and the Spell Book (K) explains every ability.
