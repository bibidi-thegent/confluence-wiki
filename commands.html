Commands

Every command a player can use, in the groups /cf help shows in game, made from the same list. In a form, <player> is a value you fill in, [off|on] one you may leave out, and a|b means one of them. Hover over a line in game for the same longer text.

Play

Your kit, levels, quests, Echoes and Deeds

  • /cf kit Pick your Discipline kit

    Your kit is a Discipline: an Expertise (the class) and its spec (Tank, Damage or Healer). Swaps happen out of combat, never in the Colossus arena or a Breach room with a live pack.

    • /cf kit Open the Discipline picker

      Every Discipline with its readiness, in a dialog (or the chest menu under vendor.menu: gui).

    • /cf kit list List every Discipline

      Every Discipline with its readiness, as text.

    • /cf kit <discipline> Play a Discipline

      A Discipline id, or an Expertise id for the Discipline you play of it; out of combat.

  • /cf expertise Swap your Expertise

    An Expertise is a class with two Disciplines; each Expertise levels on its own.

    • /cf expertise List the Expertises

      Every Expertise with its resource and its Disciplines.

    • /cf expertise <expertise> Swap to an Expertise

      To the Discipline you play of it, out of combat.

  • /cf loadout Choose your abilities

    Your Discipline's core abilities, utilities and Talent path (the Spell Book's rows do it too).

    • /cf loadout Open the loadout panel

      Pick the abilities out of combat.

    • /cf loadout reset Back to the default preset

      Your Discipline's default preset again; a refused reset keeps the pending edit.

      Acts at once: it cannot be taken back.

  • /cf mastery Your level-20 Mastery

    At level 20 each Expertise picks a stat boost; talent is the command's old name and still works.

    Also: /cf talent

    • /cf mastery List your Masteries

      The Masteries of your active Expertise and the one picked.

    • /cf mastery <mastery> Pick a Mastery

      From level 20, out of combat; the Drillmaster picks it too.

  • /cf level [player] Your levels, or a player's

    Your Core level and every Expertise's level and XP, or an online player's.

  • /cf quest Contracts: accept, track, turn in

    The contract board's dailies, weeklies and Trials. /cf quest alone is /cf quest list.

    • /cf quest list Active and available contracts

      Your active contracts with their progress, then the ones on offer and who gives them.

    • /cf quest accept <id> Accept a contract

      A contract on offer, while you hold fewer than the most you may.

    • /cf quest track <id> Track a contract

      An active contract's progress on your HUD.

    • /cf quest untrack Track nothing

      Clears the tracked contract.

    • /cf quest abandon <id> Drop a contract

      An active contract (some cannot be dropped).

    • /cf quest turnin <id> Turn in a delivery

      Hands a delivery contract's items over.

    • /cf quest reroll Reroll today's dailies

      Once a day.

  • /cf quests Open the quest log

    Your contracts in the quest log's window.

  • /cf profile reset Start over at level 1

    Every Expertise back to level 1 with its starting kit, your contracts reset; the drills begin again. Your own profile only.

    Acts at once: it cannot be taken back.

  • /cf cosmetics Your cosmetics: wear, take off

    Everything you own, however it came: bought from the Curator, bought with Echoes, earned by a Deed or a streak. /cf cosmetics alone is /cf cosmetics list.

    • /cf cosmetics list Your wallet and wardrobe

      What you own, what is worn of each kind, and each piece's id.

    • /cf cosmetics wear <id> Wear a cosmetic

      One of each kind at a time; equip works too.

      Also: /cf cosmetics equip <id>

    • /cf cosmetics takeoff <kind> Take one off

      A kind (aura, title, dye or kill) or a worn piece's id; unequip works too.

      Also: /cf cosmetics unequip <kind>

  • /cf echoes Your Echoes and streak

    The count, the streak and the next bonus day, this week's raffle entries, your titles.

  • /cf deeds [id|category] Your Deeds, or one Deed

    The list by category with your progress, your Deed Points and titles; a category narrows it, an id shows one Deed.

  • /cf hub Go to the hub

    Teleports you to the hub.

  • /cf arena Go to the training arena

    The Colossus arena, to practise.

Breach

Parties, runs, raids, gear and the boards

  • /cf party Group up for a Breach

    A party enters a Breach together and shares gathering XP. /cf party alone is /cf party list.

    • /cf party invite <player> Invite a player

      An online player; inviting starts a party when you have none.

      Who: Anyone not in a party, or the party's leader.

    • /cf party accept Accept an invite

      Joins the party that invited you.

    • /cf party decline Decline an invite

      Turns the waiting invite down.

    • /cf party leave Leave your party

      When the leader leaves, the party gets a new one.

    • /cf party kick <player> Remove a member

      An online member of your party.

      Who: The party's leader.

    • /cf party list Your party's members

      The members and the leader.

    • /cf party disband End the party

      Every member leaves at once.

      Who: The party's leader.

      Acts at once: it cannot be taken back.

    • /cf party share [off|on] Share gathering XP

      The party's gathering XP share (on by default); alone, whether it is on.

      Who: A member sees it; the party's leader switches it.

  • /cf dungeon Enter, leave and check a Breach

    Breach runs and their vendors. /cf dungeon alone is /cf dungeon status.

    • /cf dungeon enter <tier> Enter a Breach tier

      Pays the entry and takes you in, your party with you; alone, the tiers on offer. An op may enter a scenario's tier to test it.

      Who: Anyone alone; in a party, its leader.

    • /cf dungeon leave Leave your run

      Out of the Breach you are in.

    • /cf dungeon status Your run and balance

      The run you are in and your balance.

    • /cf dungeon shop [gui|dialog] Buy a Breach entry

      The Breach Vendor's shop: the configured menu, or the chest menu (gui) or the dialog.

    • /cf dungeon outfitter [gui|dialog] Buy gear

      The Outfitter's shop: class pieces and Inscribe.

    • /cf dungeon curator [gui|dialog] Buy cosmetics

      The Curator's shop.

  • /cf meter Your run's damage and healing

    The live run's breakdown so far while you are in one, else your last run's again.

  • /cf raid lockouts Your lockouts this week

    Your rooms this week and when they reset; status works too.

    Also: /cf raid status

  • /cf salvage Break gear down into materials

    Breach gear into materials: a preview line first, then confirm.

    • /cf salvage hand Salvage the held piece

      Not the weapon in slot 1.

    • /cf salvage all Salvage every loose piece

      Every unequipped gear piece in the grid and hotbar except slot 1.

    • /cf salvage confirm Confirm the salvage

      Within the confirm window after the preview.

  • /cf upgrade Raise a piece's Item Power

    The held Breach piece's next step: a preview, then confirm.

    • /cf upgrade hand Preview the held piece's step

      What the next step costs and gives.

    • /cf upgrade confirm Confirm the upgrade

      Within the confirm window after the preview.

  • /cf vault Open your Breach vault

    Your Breach gear, kept off your bag: Breach gear you pick up and Breach chest pieces land there.

  • /cf pack Your backpacks

    Shulker boxes worn as packs, counted from 1; the mod's Packs tab is their window. /cf pack alone is /cf pack list.

    • /cf pack list Your packs and their switches

      Each pack's box, how full it is and its overflow switch.

    • /cf pack equip <slot> Equip the held box

      As that pack; a box already there goes back to your hand.

    • /cf pack unequip <slot> Take a pack off

      With everything in it, into your bag.

    • /cf pack open <slot> Open a pack

      The pack's window.

    • /cf pack overflow <slots> <on|off> Overflow switch

      With overflow on, what you pick up with a full bag flows into that pack.

  • /cf gear Your gear's look and your two kits

    Your gear pieces and your two kits (the Breach kit and the survival kit).

    • /cf gear refresh Rebuild your gear's look

      Every gear piece you carry rebuilt on the current band body, colour and trim; a worn dye is kept.

    • /cf gear kits Your Breach and survival kits

      One line per slot: which is in the inventory and which is stored.

  • /cf worldboss The world boss pylons

    Shards deposited at the hub's pylons summon the world boss. /cf worldboss alone is /cf worldboss status.

    • /cf worldboss status The pylons' pool

      What each pylon holds and how far off the summon is.

    • /cf worldboss deposit <1-max> Deposit at a pylon

      Your Shards at that pylon (with the Confluence mod).

  • /cf top The hub scoreboards

    The Records' boards: levels, clears and more.

    • /cf top Every board and its leader

      The boards with the player on top of each.

    • /cf top <board> One board's places

      Rank, name and value, and a fastest clear's party.

Town

Towns, plots, locks and your home

  • /cf town Found and run a town

    Towns in the towns' world: claims, members, assistants, the spawn and the farm settings. Assistants claim, release, invite, kick, move the spawn, edit and set the settings like the mayor.

    • /cf town create <name> Found a town here

      On the chunks around you, in the towns' world; the name, the distance from other towns and the cost are checked.

      Who: A player in no town.

    • /cf town info [name] A town's mayor, members and plots

      Yours (or the one you stand in), or any by name: the mayor, members, assistants, plots and settings.

    • /cf town list Every town

      Each town with its mayor, members and chunks.

    • /cf town claim Claim this chunk

      Next to the town, under the limit, apart from other towns; the one who claims pays.

      Who: The mayor or an assistant.

    • /cf town unclaim Release this chunk

      Nothing is refunded.

      Who: The mayor or an assistant.

      Acts at once: it cannot be taken back.

    • /cf town invite <player> Invite a player

      An online player in no town.

      Who: The mayor or an assistant.

    • /cf town join <town> Join a town that invited you

      The town's invite must be waiting.

      Who: A player in no town, with an invite.

    • /cf town leave Leave your town

      The mayor cannot leave; delete the town instead.

      Who: A member other than the mayor.

    • /cf town kick <player> Remove a member

      An assistant never removes the mayor or another assistant.

      Who: The mayor or an assistant.

    • /cf town assistant <player> Make a member an assistant

      An assistant does the mayor's work except naming assistants and deleting the town.

      Who: The mayor.

    • /cf town unassistant <player> Remove an assistant

      The member keeps their place in the town.

      Who: The mayor.

    • /cf town setspawn Move the town's spawn here

      On the town's own land.

      Who: The mayor or an assistant.

    • /cf town spawn [name] Go to your town, or any

      Your town's spawn, or a town's by name.

    • /cf town delete Disband your town

      Every member is told; the land is free again.

      Who: The mayor.

      Acts at once: it cannot be taken back.

    • /cf town wilds Go to the wilds

      A safe spot in the wilds around the hub, once per cooldown; frontier works too.

      Also: /cf town frontier

    • /cf town edit Toggle edit mode

      The town's outline around you while it is on; claims and releases follow the usual rules.

      Who: The mayor or an assistant.

    • /cf town chat [message] Town chat; alone toggles it

      A line to your town; alone, sticky town chat on or off (/t does the same).

      Who: A member of the town.

    • /cf town mobs [off|on] Keep hostile mobs out

      off keeps hostile mobs out of the town, on lets them back; alone, the town's setting.

      Who: A member sees it; the mayor or an assistant sets it.

    • /cf town trample [off|on] Stop farmland trampling

      off: nobody tramples the town's farmland, members included; alone, the town's setting.

      Who: A member sees it; the mayor or an assistant sets it.

    • /cf town crops [shared|owner] Who harvests plot crops

      shared: any member harvests on every owned plot; owner: only those who may build on it (its owner, the players they allowed, the mayor and the assistants). The town's own land keeps the build rule either way.

      Who: A member sees it; the mayor or an assistant sets it.

    • /cf town stands [shared|owner] Who gets brewing XP

      shared: a brewing stand in the town pays any member's brews (Alchemy's XP and perks); owner: only its placer's.

      Who: A member sees it; the mayor or an assistant sets it.

    • /cf town outpost A detached 3 x 3 claim

      One per town: a 3 x 3 of chunks that need not touch the town, beside its chunk limit, with its own four settings. No spawn of its own.

      • /cf town outpost buy Buy the 3 x 3 around you

        Stand in the centre chunk; none of the nine may touch the town or lie near another town. The one who buys pays.

        Who: The mayor or an assistant.

      • /cf town outpost release Give the outpost back

        The whole 3 x 3 goes, its plots with it; nothing is refunded and the town may buy another.

        Who: The mayor or an assistant.

        Acts at once: it cannot be taken back.

      • /cf town outpost info Centre, settings and plots

        Where your town's outpost stands, its plots and its four settings, or what one costs.

        Who: A member of the town.

      • /cf town outpost mobs [off|on] Keep hostile mobs out

        As /cf town mobs, for the outpost alone; alone, its setting.

        Who: A member sees it; the mayor or an assistant sets it.

      • /cf town outpost trample [off|on] Stop trampling

        As /cf town trample, for the outpost alone; alone, its setting.

        Who: A member sees it; the mayor or an assistant sets it.

      • /cf town outpost crops [shared|owner] Who harvests

        As /cf town crops, for the outpost's plots alone; alone, its setting.

        Who: A member sees it; the mayor or an assistant sets it.

      • /cf town outpost stands [shared|owner] Brewing XP

        As /cf town stands, for the outpost's brewing stands alone; alone, its setting.

        Who: A member sees it; the mayor or an assistant sets it.

  • /cf plot Sell, buy or share the plot you stand on

    A plot is the chunk you stand on, in a town. /cf plot alone is /cf plot info.

    • /cf plot forsale [price] Put the plot up for sale

      At the price given, or the default; a sale pays the plot's owner, or the mayor for town land.

      Who: The plot's owner, the mayor or an assistant.

    • /cf plot notforsale Take it off the market

      The sale ends.

      Who: The plot's owner, the mayor or an assistant.

    • /cf plot buy Buy the plot

      A plot for sale in your own town.

      Who: A member of the town.

    • /cf plot info Owner, sale and builders

      The plot's town, owner, price and who may build on it.

    • /cf plot give <player> Give the plot to a member

      It becomes theirs.

      Who: The plot's owner, the mayor or an assistant.

    • /cf plot allow <player> Let a player build here

      On an owned plot: any player who has joined the server, online or not.

      Who: The plot's owner, the mayor or an assistant.

    • /cf plot deny <player> Take building rights back

      By the name on the plot's list.

      Who: The plot's owner, the mayor or an assistant.

    • /cf plot evict Return the plot to town land

      The former owner hears it, and so does the town; an assistant never evicts the mayor or another assistant.

      Who: The mayor or an assistant.

      Acts at once: it cannot be taken back.

  • /cf lock Lock doors and containers to you

    Containers, doors and trapdoors: the next right-click within the arm window takes the action.

    • /cf lock Lock or unlock the next one

      Arms a lock: right-click a container, door or trapdoor (an unlocked one, or one of yours) within the window.

    • /cf lock add <player> [persist] Share the next one

      Anyone who has joined, online or not; persist keeps sharing every one you right-click until you type it again.

    • /cf lock remove <player> [persist] Stop sharing one

      Takes the player off the next one; persist keeps doing it until you type it again.

    • /cf lock list Your locks

      Each lock with where it stands and who shares it.

    • /cf lock persist Keep locking every click

      Every one you right-click is locked (or unlocked) until you type it again or it sits idle too long.

  • /home Your one home: go, set, remove

    One home in the survival worlds. From the hub and the Quarry /home goes at once; elsewhere it warms up, and moving or damage cancels it. An op's /home always goes at once.

    • /home Go home

      From the survival worlds, their Nether and End, the hub and the Quarry.

    • /home set Set your home here

      In a survival world; replacing an old home asks first.

    • /home set confirm Confirm a replacement

      Within the time the replace line names.

    • /home remove Remove your home

      No home until you set one again.

      Acts at once: it cannot be taken back.

Survival

Professions, market, wilds, Quarry, gates

  • /cf skill <profession> Start a profession's Prime

    As the profession tool's right-click does.

  • /cf professions Your profession levels

    Every profession's level, with your Crop Sense and Senses.

  • /cf market The market hall's vendors

    Buy and sell at the current prices; a sale goes to the market that buys the item. The Chronicler buys the finds.

    • /cf market Open the Smith

      The Smith's menu: blocks, materials and tools at the current prices.

    • /cf market <vendors> Browse

      A market vendor's wares; each has a booth in the market hall.

    • /cf market sell <hand|all> Sell the held stack or all

      hand: the held stack at the market that buys it; all: every sellable stack at every market that buys it, with a summary per market.

    • /cf market price <item> An item's price

      The current price at the market that sells the item; for a find (find:relic_shard) it says the Chronicler buys it.

    • /cf market stock <item> An item's stock

      How much the market that sells the item holds.

  • /cf auction The Auctioneer: players' work

    Players sell each other made things (enchanted, brewed, built or designed) at their own price; browse by category and buy at the Auctioneer in the market hall.

    • /cf auction Open the Auctioneer

      The catalog by category, your listings, Collect and Post, from anywhere in the hub.

    • /cf auction post <price> List the held stack

      The stack in your hand at that price in whole Yen for the whole stack (5000, 5,000 or 5k), in the hub; made things only.

    • /cf auction cancel <n> Cancel a listing

      Your listing with that number in /cf auction list, in the hub; it comes back to your bag, or waits at the Auctioneer.

    • /cf auction collect Collect your mailbox

      Ended, cancelled and bought stacks into your bag, in the hub; the sale notes are read out.

    • /cf auction list Your listings in chat

      Each with its price and time left, numbered for cancel.

  • /cf wilds Your Wilds Pass

    Whether you hold it.

  • /cf dragon Is the End's dragon up?

    Each survival End's dragon: alive, rising, or when it returns.

  • /cf quarry Your Quarry tickets and time

    Your Quarry Tickets, your Quarry time left, the Quarry's map and the next weekly reset.

  • /cf gates Your Nether and End gates

    Whether each is open to you.

  • /cf net About Mob Nets, and the nets you carry

    Where nets come from, what slips the net, and how many filled and empty nets your bag holds.

  • /cf trader What it sells, and its last visit

    What the trader sells, how often it comes, and the town it last came to (by name only).

  • /cf mount Ride animals and fly your phantom

    Ride your tamed wolf, or a grown cow or sheep, with a saddle in hand; fly your phantom over the main world and the frontier. The Stable Master in the hub teaches each.

    • /cf mount phantom Call your phantom and fly

      In the main world and the frontier, out of a fight, once you have its lesson; 30 s after you last sent it away.

    • /cf mount off Get off and send it away

      Off any mount; your phantom flies off, and in the air you float down.

    • /cf mount list Your riding lessons

      The kinds you can ride and how; horses, camels, pigs, striders, happy ghasts and the nautilus need no lesson.

Chat

Town chat and whispers

  • /t [message] Town chat; alone toggles it

    A line to your town's members; /t alone turns sticky town chat on or off (your chat lines go to the town until you type /t again).

    Who: A member of the town.

  • /w <player> <message> Whisper a player

    To an online player: [To Name] and [From Name].

Teleport

Go to a player, or bring one to you

  • /tpa <player> Ask to teleport to a player

    Asks an online player, by exact name, to let you come to them; they answer in chat with [Accept] or [Deny], or /tpaccept and /tpdeny. On Accept you go to where they stand. Not in a fight, nor into or out of a Breach.

  • /tpahere <player> Ask a player to come to you

    Asks an online player, by exact name, to come to you; they answer in chat with [Accept] or [Deny]. On Accept they land where you stand. Not in a fight, nor into or out of a Breach.

  • /tpaccept [player] Accept a teleport request

    Alone, the one request waiting, or a list to pick from when more wait; with a name, that player's. The rules are checked first: a refused accept leaves the request waiting.

  • /tpdeny [player] Decline a teleport request

    Alone, the one request waiting, or a list to pick from when more wait; with a name, that player's. The asker is told, and waits a while before asking you again.

  • /tptoggle [off|on] Teleport requests on or off

    Alone it flips; off or on sets it. Off, nobody can ask you and the asker is told so; requests already waiting stay answerable. Kept across logins; an op's /cf tphere still reaches you.
