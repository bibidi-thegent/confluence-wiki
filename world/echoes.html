Echoes

Echoes: once a day, walk to the Calendar on the east grass south of the plaza's east arm (a copper wall with a dark board of cells, facing the plaza) and step onto its amethyst sill, or right-click the board: you claim the day's Echo ("An Echo: 12 Echoes, a 4-day streak."). Echoes live on your character, never in your bag. A day turns at 04:00 server time; a second touch says when the next one comes. The board shows this month (Monday first, today's number in gold) and lights the days you claimed, for you alone.

Streaks: claim on days in a row. Days 7, 14, 21 and 28 of a streak pay a bonus Echo. Missing a day starts the streak over but never takes Echoes. A 7-day streak earns the title the Faithful, a 30-day streak Keeper of the Tide (wear them from the Curator's Wardrobe); they are never sold.

The Chronicler (beside the Calendar, east of the plaza) trades Echoes for prizes: 7 for a Quarry Ticket or 300 Yen; 14 for an enchanted book (from your best Breach clear's book table) or 3 Bottles o' Enchanting; 30 for any cosmetic the Curator sells that you don't own (you wear it at once; the Curator's Wardrobe, its first button, lists everything you own, however it came, with Wear and Take off, and so does /cf cosmetics list, with /cf cosmetics wear <id> and takeoff); 60 for the choice crate: three cantrip pieces at your level are shown and you keep one (the Echoes go when you pick; close it and nothing is spent). Prices are spent, nothing refunds; a full bag drops the prize at your feet.

Sunday raffle: every Echo you claim from Monday 04:00 to Sunday 20:00 is a ticket in that week's draw at 20:00 Sunday. The winner gets a Quarry Ticket and 500 Yen (at once if online, else waiting at the Chronicler) and it is posted in Discord. /cf echoes shows your Echoes, streak, next bonus day, raffle entries and titles.
