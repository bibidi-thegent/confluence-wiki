Deeds

Deeds: Confluence's achievements. Press L (Advancements) and open the Confluence Deeds tab: one icon per Deed, lit when earned, grey for what comes next. Earning one pops the toast and says "Deed earned: First Step (+10 points)". 28 in all: Breach clears (your first, the Warden's Breach, one with no deaths, one in each role), kills in Breaches (100, 1,000, 10,000), the Manor and the Curator's chests, profession levels (10, 25, 50, and the five gathering ones at 25), blocks, finds and ten hunting trophies, a night survived in the wilds (dusk to dawn, no dying or sleeping), the Wilds Pass, the dragon, the Nether Gate, 1,000 Quarry blocks, founding a town, a Breach in a party of four, healing, all sixty pages of the Chronicle, ten incursions answered and the Astronomer's Observatory cleared (The Other Sky). Three are secret: nothing in the tab and only "???" in /cf deeds until you stumble into them.

Rewards: Deed Points (10, 25 or 50 a Deed) on the new Deed Points board on the north-west terrace (/cf top deeds), and four titles nobody can buy: the Breacher (your first Breach clear), Skitter Stomper (100 Breach kills), Gate Breaker (the Warden's Breach) and the Attentive (Reader: the whole Chronicle); wear them at the Curator. No Yen or tokens. The rare ones are announced to the server and in Discord.

Already done it? Your levels, the Wilds Pass, the Nether Gate and the Breaches you have cleared count at your first login, and so do your Breach kills. /cf deeds lists every Deed with your progress ("307/1,000"), your points and titles; /cf deeds <id> shows one.
