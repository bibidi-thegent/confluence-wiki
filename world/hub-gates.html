The world

Hub gates

Hub gates: two more arches stand in the World Gate's row at the end of the plaza's west arm. The Nether Gate (obsidian with crying obsidian and soul lanterns, just south of the World Gate) opens for good for one Ghast Tear: trade it to the Town Warden ("Open the Nether Gate: 1 Ghast Tear"; a tear in your survival hotbar slot 1 counts too). Its sill takes you to a landing room in the Nether, built on solid ground with an obsidian floor walled in from the lava: the frame on its north wall brings you back to the hub, and the door in its south wall, behind you as you arrive, opens onto a porch and the Nether's own ground. Until then the sill only says "Sealed. The Warden opens it for a Ghast Tear." The End Gate (end stone and purpur, just north of the World Gate, its sill three lit portal frames) opens for Wilds Pass holders and for anyone in a party whose holder is online, checked each time you step on: you land on the End's obsidian platform, so a holder can take friends to the dragon for eggs of their own. The End's exit portal brings you home the vanilla way, and /cf hub always works. Neither gate touches your bag. /cf gates shows both.

Hub signs: floating names hang over the hub's places: each gate's name just under the top of its arch, in front of it and facing the way in (World, Quarry, Nether, End and Raid Gate), with a line on where it goes under it (the gates' signs are gone); The Records on the north-west terrace; and The Market, The Breaches, The Town Portals, The Arena and Combat Training over the bridges where they leave the central island, turning to face you. Under The Records, a small catalog is sunk into the terrace: right-click its lectern to read "The Records", every board's top ten, kept current; crouch (C in combat mode) and right-click it for the server's copy of the Chronicle.
