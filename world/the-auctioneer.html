The world

The Auctioneer

Where: the villager at the back counter of the market hall sells what players make, at the price they set. Right-click it, or /cf auction from anywhere in the hub.

What sells: made things, not plain goods: anything enchanted, potions, tipped arrows, enchanted books, shulker boxes (empty or full), fireworks, banners with patterns, shields with a banner, written books, maps, anything renamed at an anvil, trimmed or dyed armour. Plain stacks are for the market vendors; Breach gear, Mob Nets, finds and anything else of Confluence's never sell here.

Selling: say you put Efficiency IV and Unbreaking III on a diamond pickaxe. Click Post, click the pickaxe in your bag, type your price for the whole stack (5000, 5,000 or 5k) and Post. It stays up 7 days, and you can have 10 up at once. No fees: the buyer's Yen lands in your wallet whole, even while you are offline, and you hear who bought it.

Buying: pick a category (Tools, Weapons, Armour, Enchanted books, Potions and brews, Blocks, Materials, Redstone, Food, Misc, or All), hover a listing for its full tooltip with the price, the seller, the time left and the item's real name, then click it and Buy. Sort by newest, ending soon, price or price per item.

Your mailbox: a listing that did not sell, a cancel, or a buy your bag had no room for waits at the Auctioneer: click Collect. My listings cancels one.

/cf auction list shows yours; /cf auction post <price> lists the stack in your hand.
