The world

Chest titles

Chest titles: right-click a chest, barrel or copper chest with a Name Tag. One named at an anvil gives its name at once; a plain one opens a window to type it (30 characters, about 25 letters) with a switch to show it on the chest. The title replaces "Large Chest" in the window; sneak + right-click (or the switch) hangs it on the front, two lines at most, shrunk to fit. A new title uses up the tag; the label and Clear are free. A locked chest is its owner's to name.

Dump chests: tick "Dump chest" in that window (free), then hang item frames holding a sample on the chests to fill. Sneak + right-click the dump with an empty hand: each item goes to the nearest framed chest for its type with room; the rest stays. On town land it feeds the town's storage, on a plot its owner's plots within 9 chunks, in the wilds chests within 16 blocks; never a chest you can't open.
