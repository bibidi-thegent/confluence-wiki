The world

Teleports

Ask: /tpa <name> asks to go to a player, /tpahere <name> asks them to come to you. They get a chat line with [Accept] and [Deny] (or type /tpaccept and /tpdeny, adding the name when several wait). On Accept the one who moves goes at once and lands beside the other. A request lapses after 60 s; after a Deny you wait 60 s to ask that player again. One request out at a time: a new one withdraws your last.

Where: the survival worlds, their Nether, the hub; the End only with a Wilds Pass (yours or a party member's); the Quarry only with Quarry time of your own (a request never spends a ticket). Never into or out of a Breach, and not in a fight.

Off: /tptoggle (or /tptoggle off and on) stops everyone asking you; it stays that way across logins. /cf help teleport lists the commands.

Warps: /warp set <name> marks a place of your own where you stand, in the survival worlds, their Nether or their End: five of them, each name up to 24 characters (spaces allowed) and one of a kind across the server. /warp <name> goes like /home: hold still 3 s, at once from the hub or the Quarry, never from a Breach; a warp in the End needs the Wilds Pass (yours or a party member's). /warp list (or /warp alone) shows yours and the ones shared with you, each with a [Go] button; /warp menu shows them as buttons. /warp invite <name> <player> offers one of yours and /warp request <name> asks a warp's owner (both online): they answer with [Accept] or [Deny] within 60 s. Shared warps never count toward your five. /warp leave <name> drops one you were given; the owner can /warp remove <name> <player> or /warp delete <name>. Your own and shared warps show on the map as pennants (Guide here only); a very full map keeps, in order, your waypoints, your warps, shared warps, the party's, the Seam's, dropping the last.
