The world

Teleports

Ask: /tpa <name> asks to go to a player, /tpahere <name> asks them to come to you. They get a chat line with [Accept] and [Deny] (or type /tpaccept and /tpdeny, adding the name when several wait). On Accept the one who moves goes at once and lands beside the other. A request lapses after 60 s; after a Deny you wait 60 s to ask that player again. One request out at a time: a new one withdraws your last.

Where: the survival worlds, their Nether, the hub; the End only with a Wilds Pass (yours or a party member's); the Quarry only with Quarry time of your own (a request never spends a ticket). Never into or out of a Breach, and not in a fight.

Off: /tptoggle (or /tptoggle off and on) stops everyone asking you; it stays that way across logins. /cf help teleport lists the commands.
