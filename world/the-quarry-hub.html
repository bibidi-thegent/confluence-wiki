The world

The Quarry hub

The Quarry hub: a sky platform ringed by fourteen portals, one per biome: Desert, Badlands, Forest, Taiga, Jungle, Swamp, Mountains, Ocean, Savanna, Snowy, Cherry, Dark Forest, Mushroom and Lush Caves, each built from its biome's blocks. Step into one to land on a pad in the middle of a big patch of that biome (the Ocean's on a beach, the Lush Caves' in a room underground); the frame on the pad brings you back to the platform, and the frame in the platform's middle takes you home. A portal with a black glass sill and "Not in this Quarry" on its sign leads nowhere: that biome is not near enough on this map. The platform and the pads cannot be dug, built on, reshaped with a tool or bone meal, or blown up; falling off the platform does not hurt. Look up from the ground under it: the platform rides on a chunk of earth and rock torn out of the land, roots and vines trailing from it, a few clods drifting beside it and a glow in amethyst at its tip. Over the middle frame's opening a sign reads "This ground is a copy. Look up."; once you have taken chapter 7 (The Torn Ground), the glow at the tip blinks and sends up sparks for you alone.
