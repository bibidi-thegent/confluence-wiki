Confluence Caches

Where and when: while you play in the overworld wilds (the main world or the frontier), a cache lands 40 to 120 m from you every 45 to 90 minutes of active play. AFK time, the hub, a Breach, the Quarry, the Nether and the End do not count, nor does the time while your last cache stands; a fight holds a due one back.

The news: chat and your HUD say "A Confluence Cache landed 80 m north-east of you." Anyone else within 160 m hears it too, from their own spot, and gets a pennant on the map (M), "Confluence Cache (the Seam)": right-click it and Guide here for the arrow and the beam. Nobody else is told.

The crater: a small bowl of end stone and coarse dirt with a chest sunk at its centre, a label with the minutes left and a smoke column you can follow from 160 m. Nobody can dig, build in or blow up the bowl, and no hopper reaches the chest.

First come, first served: one cache for whoever reaches it: everyone sees the same slots and the first click wins. Take only; nothing goes in. Always a Mob Net (sometimes two), often a purse of Yen that pays your wallet when you take it, and two to four more: experience bottles, leads, a name tag, a saddle, an enchanted book, ingots, redstone, lapis, ender pearls, arrows, now and then a Quarry Ticket or a market find.

The fade: 20 minutes after it lands, or 30 seconds once emptied, it goes in a puff: the ground comes back as it was, what is left inside is lost, and anyone in the bowl is lifted onto the ground. A restart fades every cache.

Never: in or beside a town, by a home or a bed, near the Waystation, the hub or the world spawn, by an incursion or another cache, on a build, in water or under trees.
