The world

The post office

Death chests: die in the survival world and your bag goes into a death chest at the nearest safe spot, its timer over it: how long until it is fair game, then until it is mailed. It is yours alone for 45 minutes; in the last 15 anyone may take from it (what they take is theirs, and you are told). At 60 minutes the rest goes to the post office, free. Right-click it to take your things back; nothing goes in. No safe spot (the void, lava): straight to the post office.

The Postmaster has a stall on the town portal island, south of the court. Collect takes your free mail into your bag (what does not fit waits). Collect early takes your death stacks before their hour is up for a fine: ¥500 + ¥100 a stack, at most ¥5,000. Walking back to the chest is free.

Send: click Send a parcel, then a stack in your bag, then name the player (online or not) and add a note if you like: ¥100 postage. No Breach gear, Mob Nets or anything else of Confluence's.

Breach leftovers: loot with no room in your bag when you leave a run, shares you never opened after a disconnect, and a relic left on the floor are mailed to you.

The Auctioneer mails here too: an unsold listing, a cancel, or a buy your bag had no room for.

You have mail: a line when you log in, a toast when it arrives, "Post Office (3)" on the hub map and a small gold envelope with the count at the top left of your screen.

/cf mail list shows your mail anywhere; /cf mail collect, collect early and send <player> [note] work in the hub.
