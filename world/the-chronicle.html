The world

The Chronicle

The Chronicle: the Chronicler keeps a book of sixty short pages about the Confluence, the tears and the Hollow on the other side. Each Echo you claim at the Calendar turns your next page: it shows in chat in gold ("The Chronicle, page 7: ...") and as a notice. Everyone starts at page 1, whatever they claimed before.

Reading it: at the Chronicler, "The Chronicle" (first on his list) opens every page you have read as a book on your screen, each leaf's name over its first page and "Pages read: 7 of 60" at the end. Crouch (C in combat mode) and right-click the lectern under The Records for the server's copy: every page up to the furthest anyone has turned. A plain click still opens The Records.

Reader: read all sixty to earn the Deed Reader (+25 points, posted in Discord) and the title the Attentive, never sold. Once you have read the last page, the Calendar still pays its Echo every day.

Pages given early: with chapter 2 done, the Chronicler's The second tear hands you page 14, every incursion you help drive back hands you your next page, and your first Cellar or Observatory clear each week hands you page 58 or 60 (or your next page). The Calendar skips a page you already hold.

Chapters: the Breach Board's chapters have new names and end with a line from the story. Chapter 7, The Torn Ground, opens at level 25 once chapter 6 is done: spend a Quarry Ticket at the Quarry Gate and step in (25,000 XP, 200 Breach Tokens). Chapter 8, The Other Sky, follows it at 25: take it, then lead a party of 2 to 4, all through chapters 1 to 7, into the Astronomer's Observatory from the Raid Gate. It pays 40,000 XP and the title Seam-Seer. The chapters leave marks: a plaque by the Breach Gate and one by the World Gate, a line over the Arena, a sign on the Quarry platform, and a word from the Drillmaster and the Town Warden on chapters 3 and 4.
