The world

Towns

Assistants and plots: a mayor can name assistants (/cf town assistant <name>). Only they build on town land; on a plot only its owner and the players they /cf plot allow. Standing on a plot, the mayor or an assistant can take it back for the town with /cf plot evict (nothing is refunded).

Settings: the mayor and assistants set four things, and /cf town info shows them. /cf town mobs off removes hostile mobs from the town, endermen and spiders included, and no enderman can teleport in; a mob that attacks you there is removed on sight, and only your own hit spares it for 8 s (on lets them back). /cf town trample off stops all crop trampling (on again). /cf town crops shared lets every member harvest the crops on any plot (owner again). /cf town stands shared lets every member's brews at the town's brewing stands earn Alchemy XP (owner again).

Outpost: a town can buy one outpost, a 3 x 3 of chunks away from the town, for ¥22,500: the mayor or an assistant stands in its centre chunk and types /cf town outpost buy. It may not touch the town or come within 8 chunks of another town. It does not count toward the town's 64 chunks, and the town cannot grow out from it. Its chunks are plots like any other.

Outpost settings: it starts with the town's four settings and keeps its own after that: /cf town outpost mobs off, and the same for trample, crops and stands. /cf town outpost info shows them. /cf town outpost release gives the whole 3 x 3 back (nothing refunded; its plots go with it). It has no spawn of its own: walk, ride or set a waypoint.
