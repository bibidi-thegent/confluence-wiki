The world

Waypoints

Waypoints: on the map (M), right-click a spot to set one there: the map closes and a box asks for its name (up to 24 characters; it offers "Waypoint 1", then the next free number). It stands on the map as a small gold pennant with its name under it. You can hold 16 in each world, the hub and the Quarry included. They live on your profile on the server, so a reinstall or another PC keeps them.

The menu: right-click a pennant (or its name) for Guide here (Stop guiding on the guided one), Rename, Share (Unshare), Remove and Cancel. Esc closes the menu, not the map.

The guide: one target at a time. Right of your hotbar, over the COMBAT/SURVIVAL tag, a small arrow turns with you to point at it, with its name and the distance on the level ("Iron vein 340 m"); in the hub and the Breaches it sits right of your health bar. A tall column of light stands at the spot, seen through hills and walls, fading past 150 m and gone at 300 m; up close it fades out too (from 8 m, gone within 3 m), so it never covers your view at the spot. On the map the guided pennant has a white ring, and when it is off the map an arrow on the edge points at it.

Your death: with no guide in your world, the arrow and the light point at your death marker ("Your death 340 m") until you walk within 8 m of it. To clear it sooner, right-click its skull on the map and answer Remove (your items stay where they fell), or Waypoint here to mark the spot.

Sharing: Share shows a waypoint to your party members in that world, in your party colour with your name after it ("Iron vein (Vex)"), and they can guide to it. Only you can rename, share or remove yours. It goes from their maps when you unshare or remove it or the party ends, and a guide to it ends with it.

The Quarry: its waypoints go each time the Torn Ground re-forms.
