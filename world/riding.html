The world

Riding

The Stable Master (the hub's training island, by the yard) teaches one lesson per animal, yours for good: cow ¥500, sheep ¥500, wolf ¥1,000, phantom ¥5,000. Your first lesson comes with a saddle. Horses, camels and the other vanilla mounts need no lesson.

Get on: hold a saddle and right-click a grown cow or sheep, or your own tamed, grown wolf, not crouching. The saddle may be in your off hand beside an empty main hand or your Breach weapon, so combat mode works too. It is not used up. Not one on someone else's lead or with a rider, nor the hub's; on town land only with the town's leave.

Steer: W, A, S and D go the way you look, the sprint key (not a double-tap of W) about a third faster, Space jumps, and it steps up a whole block. The server moves it, so it answers a beat late. Crouch (C in combat mode) or /cf mount off gets you off, and the animal is itself again. Your head under water gets you off too.

The phantom: /cf mount phantom calls it under you. It flies where you look: W to go, Space to climb, crouch to sink; let go and it hovers. To get off, land, let go of crouch and press it again: a crouch held from the air never drops you. /cf mount off works anywhere; in the air you float down with no fall damage. Nobody else can climb on. It rests 30 s after it leaves.

Where: the main world and the frontier, never the Nether, the End, the hub, the Breaches or the Quarry. An incursion's ring and the hub's grounds are walls; the phantom also meets a ceiling over the build limit and the edge of its flying area, 4,000 m from spawn.

Fights: no mounting or calling in a fight, and a fight puts you down. Evade or a cast gets you off a walking mount (no charge spent); on the phantom they wait: "Land first." A knockback now takes you off any mount, boat or horse.

/cf mount list shows your lessons and how to ride each.
