The world

Locks and benches

Lock it: /cf lock, then right-click a chest, barrel, furnace, door or trapdoor (any container): only you open it. /cf lock add <name> then a right-click shares it, /cf lock remove <name> takes that back, /cf lock list shows yours. Five locks in the wilds; inside your own town's claim as many as you like.

Keep locking: /cf lock persist locks everything you right-click until you type it again (or after 10 minutes without a lock). It never unlocks, and blocks it cannot lock work as usual. /cf lock add <name> persist shares every lock of yours you right-click the same way (remove instead of add takes it back).

Doors: redstone and mobs cannot open a locked door. A locked wooden or copper door you open shuts by itself 3 s later. Lock both doors of a double door and they open and shut together.

Benches: crouch and right-click a stair or a slab (the right way up) with both hands empty, or with your Breach weapon in combat mode, then let go of crouch to sit. Crouch or jump to stand up. Not in a fight.
