The world

Mob Nets

Catch: hold an empty Mob Net and right-click a mob: it goes into the net whole, with its saddle, armour, a horse's colour and markings, a donkey's chest, its name, health and a villager's trades. The net turns into the mob's egg and says what it holds.

Let it out: right-click a block with the filled net: the mob comes out on that side, facing you, and stays (it never despawns). A chest or a door keeps its click unless you sneak. A dispenser fired with a filled net lets its mob out in front of it.

Unstable: each release has a 1 in 4 chance to break the net, after the mob is out, so the mob is never lost.

Where: the survival worlds, their Nether and End, only where you may build, and never on the spots the gates land you. Not in a fight; one mob a net and a second between uses.

Slips the net: bosses and Breach enemies, someone else's pet, a mob being ridden, riding or on a lead, one hunting a player or just out of a fight with one, a villager mid-trade and a trial spawner's mob. An idle hostile mob is fair game.

Getting nets: Confluence Caches drop them; /cf net counts yours. /cf trade refuses a net: drop it for a friend instead.
