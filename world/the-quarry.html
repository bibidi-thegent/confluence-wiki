The world

The Quarry

The Quarry: a Peaceful world off the hub for digging in bulk: a desert of sand, a hill of stone, a forest of logs, with no scar on the worlds you live in.

Getting in: the Quarry Gate, a sandstone arch beside the Raid Gate's terrace, north of the plaza's east arm. Step onto its sill with a Quarry Ticket in your bag: one ticket, one hour, and you arrive on the Quarry hub. The hour keeps running while you are out or logged off, and until it ends the gate lets you back in free. A bar shows the time left, with warnings at 5 and 1 minutes; at the end a dialog offers another ticket to stay, else you go to the hub with your bag. /cf quarry shows your tickets, your time and the next reset.

Tickets: from Breach chests (10% a player at Breach I, 3 points more a tier, 25% at Warden's Breach), the Manor's chest (10% in the Rehearsal, 35% in the Soiree), Excavating from 25 (a rare find in the wilds, never in the Quarry itself), and the Town Warden sells them for ¥400. They stack and trade; the Hub Market does not buy them.

Inside: no hostile mobs, no combat mode (B is refused), and nothing hurts you: falls, lava, fire, water, cactus, TNT. A fall into the void puts you back on the Quarry hub; your hunger stays full. Professions pay in full (Acrobatics aside). TNT goes off here, and only here, so Breaker Charges work. Vanilla portals do nothing and a bed sets no spawn (an anchor explodes, as in any overworld); the platform's middle frame or /cf hub leaves.

The reset: the Quarry is the Torn Ground, the Hollow's copy of ours, and every Sunday at 04:00 it re-forms from the same map: every hole filled, every tree standing, the platform and the pads where they stood. Warnings at 30, 10 and 1 minutes, in the Quarry and on Discord; anyone inside goes to the hub with their bag and keeps their time. Nothing built or left there is kept.
