The world

Scenarios

Scenarios: fixed rooms with a story, each behind its own door (never on the Breach Vendor's list). This season's weekly one is the Curator's Cellar.

Where: every Monday at 04:00 server time a second rift opens out in the wilds, 200 to 500 m from one of you and never within 150 m of an incursion, with an arch at its centre, its opening facing east. Everyone online reads "This week's incursion: the Curator's Cellar, at X, Z." in chat, the Calendar shows the same line under its month, your map (M) marks it "The Curator's Cellar", and it is posted in Discord. It closes and heals on Sunday at 22:00.

Stepping in: walk into the arch's opening. Alone, you go straight in. In a party only the leader steps in, for everyone, and only when every member is online within 48 m of the arch ("Your party is not all here: Vex."). Free, at any level; 30 minutes and 3 deaths.

Inside: a stair with a sign worth reading; the shelves, three waves of skitters among labelled barrels; the label room, four plates under four labels to step on in the right order (a wrong step puts them all out); then the Curator's desk, where his phantom and a pair of portraits wait. He does not stay to the end; the portraits do. You come out at the arch, or at the hub once it has closed.

The pay: your first clear each week pays as a Breach III run, plus a relic at Item Power 726 in your bag and a page of the Chronicle (page 58, or your next unread one). Later clears that week pay as Breach I. Each of you is paid by your own week, and the Cellar never moves your best Breach.

Labels: when the phantom leaves, each of you finds a label in your bag with your own name on it. It says "kept". Bring the label to the Chronicler: an Echo each.
