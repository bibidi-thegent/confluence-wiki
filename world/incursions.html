The world

Incursions

Incursions: the Hollow pushing through. Every 2 to 4 hours, while someone is out in the wilds (the main world or the frontier), a rift opens on open ground 150 to 350 m from one of you: never within 200 m of a town or 100 m of a home, nor near the spawn zone or the hub, and only one at a time. Everyone online reads "An incursion at X, Z: the Hollow is pushing through." in chat (a notice too, in the wilds), and it is posted in Discord.

The rift: a disc of end stone, purpur and obsidian veins 18 m across, growing over 30 s, with a column of end rods at its centre. Your map (M) marks it with a purple diamond, or an arrow at the map's edge with its distance ("Incursion 340 m"). Its ground cannot be dug, built on or blown up, and when the incursion ends it heals back to the land it covered, block for block.

The boss: once the disc has grown, the first player within 48 m brings it through: the Hollow Juggernaut, the Hollow Hexcaster (with skitters) or the Hollow Bruiser (and its twin). Breach V size, with half its health again for each extra player within 48 m when it steps through: bring friends.

The ring: within 48 m (once its boss is out, above or below it too) everyone fights in combat mode, Wilds Pass or not (B will not switch to survival there); walk out and your own mode comes back. Dying costs what it costs in the wilds.

Rewards: everyone who hit it and is online when it falls gets their next page of the Chronicle, ¥200, a trophy roll at their Hunting Knack chance (an end rod, dragon's breath or a shulker shell), a 25% chance at a Quarry Ticket and boss-kill XP (800 to 1,200). Ten incursions answered earn the Deed Breachwatch (+25 points, posted in Discord).

Unanswered: nobody within 48 m for 20 minutes, or an hour in all, and the rift heals with the boss gone and no rewards. A server restart heals a standing rift too.
