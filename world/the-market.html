The world

The market

Five vendors stand on the market island (over The Market bridge) and trade items for Yen at a price set by their stock: the more they hold, the cheaper it gets; the less, the dearer. Right-click one to browse: left-click buys 1 (shift 16), right-click sells 1 (shift 16), Q sells every one you carry. A vendor pays 0.8 of what it asks.

Hub Market (the hall): Blocks (still plain stone, cobblestone, stone bricks, deepslate, sandstone, the quartz block, the lantern, dirt, sand and gravel), Materials, Food, Mob drops, Farming, Tools and the Finds counter; its Supply Run and Ore Run dailies.

The Dyer (west of the hall, north): wool, carpet, concrete and powder, terracotta and glazed terracotta, stained glass and panes, beds, banners, candles and dyes in all sixteen colours.

The Sawyer (east, by the bridge): every wood type's logs, wood, planks, slabs, stairs, fences, gates, doors, trapdoors, buttons, plates, signs, hanging signs, shelves, sapling, leaves and boats; bamboo's mosaic and rafts.

The Mason (west, south): stone, deepslate, granite, diorite, andesite, tuff, blackstone, basalt, bricks, quartz, prismarine and copper with their slabs, stairs and walls; lanterns, chains, end rods, glowstone and shroomlight.

The Gardener (east, south): flowers, grass and ferns, moss and vines, mushrooms and roots, ground (podzol, mycelium, grass block, mud, clay, red sand) and corals.

One vendor an item: logs, planks, white wool, glass, terracotta and the oak sapling moved off the Hub Market. /cf market opens the Hub Market from anywhere, /cf market dyer (or sawyer, mason, gardener) the others; /cf market sell hand and sell all take each item to the vendor that buys it; /cf market price <item> asks its vendor.

No crafting for profit: prices are set so that crafting what you bought and selling the result never pays.
