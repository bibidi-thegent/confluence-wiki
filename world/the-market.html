The world

The market

Six vendors have booths in the market hall on the market island (over The Market bridge) and trade items for Yen at a price set by their stock: the more they hold, the cheaper it gets. Right-click one: left-click buys 1 (shift 16), right-click sells 1 (shift 16), Q sells every one you carry. A vendor pays 0.8 of what it asks.

The Smith (west wall, by the door): blocks (plain stone, cobblestone, stone bricks, deepslate, sandstone, quartz, lanterns, dirt, sand, gravel), materials and tools; the Supply Run and Ore Run dailies.

The Grocer (east wall, by the door): food, farming goods and mob drops.

The Mason (west, middle): stone, deepslate, granite, diorite, andesite, tuff, blackstone, basalt, bricks, quartz, prismarine and copper with slabs, stairs and walls; lanterns, chains, end rods, glowstone.

The Dyer (west, back): wool, carpet, concrete and powder, terracotta and glazed terracotta, stained glass and panes, beds, banners, candles and dyes in all sixteen colours.

The Gardener (east, middle): flowers, grass, moss and vines, mushrooms, ground (podzol, mud, clay, red sand) and corals.

The Sawyer (east, back): every wood type's logs, wood, planks, slabs, stairs, fences, gates, doors, trapdoors, signs, sapling, leaves and boats; bamboo's mosaic and rafts.

The Auctioneer at the back counter is where players sell each other what they made: see its own post. Finds are not sold here: the Chronicler buys them.

One vendor an item: /cf market opens the Smith from anywhere, /cf market grocer (or dyer, sawyer, mason, gardener) the others; /cf market sell hand and sell all take each item to the vendor that buys it; /cf market price <item> asks its vendor.

No crafting for profit: prices are set so that crafting what you bought and selling the result never pays.
