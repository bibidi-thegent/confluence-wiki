The world

The market

Six vendors have booths in the market hall on the market island (over The Market bridge) and trade items for Yen at a price set by their stock: the more they hold, the cheaper it gets. Right-click one: left-click buys 1 (shift 16), right-click sells 1 (shift 16), Q sells every one you carry. A vendor pays 0.8 of what it asks.

The Smith (west wall, by the door): blocks, materials, ores (raw iron, copper and gold, their blocks, nuggets; no ore blocks), buckets, and tools, weapons and armour in every tier up to netherite; the Supply Run and Ore Run dailies. Raw metals cost a little more than ingots, since Second Smelt gets more than one ingot from each.

The Grocer (east wall, by the door): food, raw meat and fish, seeds, farming goods and mob drops.

The Mason (west, middle): every stone, brick, quartz, prismarine and copper with slabs, stairs and walls; cave, Nether and End blocks; lanterns, chains, glowstone.

The Dyer (west, back): wool, carpet, concrete, terracotta, stained glass, beds, banners, candles and dyes in all sixteen colours.

The Gardener (east, middle): flowers, grass, moss and vines, mushrooms, ground (podzol, mud, clay, red sand), ice and snow, corals.

The Sawyer (east, back): every wood's logs, planks and what they make, saplings, leaves and boats; bamboo too.

Not sold: discs, sherds, trims, heads and other curios: they are found, not bought. The Auctioneer (its own post) sells what players make; the Chronicler buys finds.

One vendor an item: /cf market opens the Smith from anywhere, /cf market grocer (or dyer, sawyer, mason, gardener) the others; /cf market sell hand and sell all take each item to the vendor that buys it; /cf market price <item> asks its vendor.

No crafting for profit: prices are set so that crafting what you bought and selling the result never pays.
