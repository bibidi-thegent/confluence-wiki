The world

The travelling trader

Who: a wandering trader and its two llamas come by after every hour or so of play, near a random player. It walks to that player's town when someone is near the town's spawn, or else to a town whose spawn is within 12 chunks of them, and waits by the spawn; with neither, it stays near that player, who alone hears "A travelling trader has arrived near <player>." It stays about 40 minutes, in plain sight day and night: no more invisibility potions. The town's members hear "A travelling trader has arrived at <town>."

Trading: right-click it, in any town, member or not. It sells for Yen and buys nothing: one Mob Net at ¥10,000, two Saddles at ¥1,000, two Leads at ¥600, two Name Tags at ¥1,500, and three wares that change each visit, in bundles (blue ice, packed ice, podzol, moss, coral blocks, cherry, pale oak or mangrove saplings, torchflowers, pitcher plants, cyan dye, pointed dripstone). The stock is shared: first come, first served. A line turns grey when you cannot pay and dark grey once sold out.

Buying: a click buys one, straight into your bag (never your weapon slot), while you stand within 6 m of where it stood when you opened it. A saddle still needs the Stable Master's lesson to ride a cow, sheep or wolf.

Nets: a Mob Net in your hand nets the trader like any mob, and it keeps its stock in the net.

/cf trader says what it sells and which town the last one came to.
