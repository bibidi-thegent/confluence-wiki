2026-10-28 Feedback round 8: the market hall, the Auctioneer, the Chronicler's finds and the stable stall

The market hall

  • Every market vendor now trades inside one building: the market hall on the market island (over The Market bridge, whose sign now reads "the market hall, the Auctioneer"), rebuilt bigger with a booth and a counter for each vendor. The copper stalls outside it and the barrel shelves along its walls are gone.
  • West wall, from the door: the Smith, the Mason, the Dyer. East wall, from the door: the Grocer, the Gardener, the Sawyer. At the back counter: the Auctioneer.
  • An item frame over each vendor shows their trade: an iron pickaxe, chiselled stone bricks, cyan dye, bread, a poppy, an oak log, and a bell for the Auctioneer.
  • The Mason, the Dyer, the Gardener and the Sawyer sell what they sold before, at the same prices.

The Smith and the Grocer

  • The Hub Market is now two vendors. The Smith (the old Hub Market villager, now in a toolsmith's coat) sells blocks, materials and tools. The Grocer (a butcher) sells food, farming goods and mob drops.
  • No price changed. The Smith keeps the Hub Market's stock; the Grocer's stock starts fresh.
  • /cf market opens the Smith from anywhere (so does /cf market smith), and /cf market grocer opens the Grocer. /cf market sell hand and sell all still take each item to the vendor that buys it.
  • The Supply Run and Ore Run dailies now come from the Smith.
  • The market no longer buys finds: take them to the Chronicler (below). /cf market points you to him when you try.

The Auctioneer

  • A new vendor at the back counter of the market hall, where players sell each other what they made, at the price they choose. Right-click it, or type /cf auction anywhere in the hub.
  • What sells: made things, not plain goods. Anything enchanted, potions (splash and lingering too, and Alchemy's own brews and draughts), tipped arrows, enchanted books, shulker boxes (empty or holding survival goods), firework rockets and stars, banners with patterns, shields with a banner, anything renamed at an anvil, trimmed armour, dyed leather or wolf armour, written books, filled maps, suspicious stew, decorated pots with sherds and bundles holding goods.
  • Plain stacks (cobblestone, diamonds, bread, an unenchanted pickaxe) are for the market vendors. Breach gear, Mob Nets, finds and anything else of Confluence's never sell here, nor a shulker box or bundle holding one.
  • Posting: click Post, click the stack in your bag, type a price in whole Yen for the whole stack (5000, 5,000 or 5k; from ¥1 to ¥100,000,000) and Post. Or, in the hub, hold it and type /cf auction post <price>. A listing stays up 7 days, and you can have 10 up at once.
  • No fees: the buyer's Yen goes to the seller whole, even while the seller is offline, and the seller hears who bought it, at once or at their next login.
  • Buying: pick a category (All, Tools, Weapons, Armour, Enchanted books, Potions and brews, Blocks, Materials, Redstone, Food, Misc), 45 listings to a page. Hover one for its own tooltip plus the price, the price each in a stack, the seller, the time left and the item's real name and enchantments, so a renamed item cannot pass for another. Click it, then Buy.
  • Sort by newest, ending soon, price up, price down or price per item. You cannot buy your own listing.
  • Your mailbox: a listing that ended unsold, a cancel, or a buy your bag had no room for waits at the Auctioneer: click Collect. My listings cancels one for free; a price never changes, so cancel and post again.
  • /cf auction list shows your listings in chat, numbered, from anywhere; /cf auction cancel <n> and /cf auction collect work in the hub.

The Chronicler and the finds

  • The Chronicler now buys your finds: Relic Shards, Lore Scraps, Old Coins, Tide Pearls, Sea Glass and Pressed Flowers. With any in your bag, his menu shows Sell your finds with how many and the Yen: one click takes every one and pays once. A find in hotbar slot 1 counts too, even while your survival kit is put away in the hub.
  • Fixed prices, what the market used to pay: Relic Shard ¥2,400, Lore Scrap ¥1,200, Old Coin ¥4,000, Tide Pearl ¥3,200, Sea Glass ¥960, Pressed Flower ¥2,000.
  • The first time you bring each kind, he tells you what it is to him. The other side's things fall through to this one, and he keeps them.
  • Sell your finds never takes Quarry Tickets, labels or trophies; a label still goes to him through Hand over a label for an Echo. A sale to him is not a market sale, so it does not count for a market contract.
  • Finds you already hold still stack with new ones.

The stable stall

  • The Stable Master's stall moved from the training island to the Town Portals island: north-east of the portal court, beside the path from the bridge, its open front toward the path. The lessons and their prices are the same.

No new kit needed

  • This update is all on the server: your current jar works.
  • The server restarts once for this update, and the hub is rebuilt.

2026-10-28 Feedback round 7: the travelling trader, town outposts, the mob ban and the chest wheel

Travelling trader

  • The wandering trader is now a travelling trader. One comes by after every hour or so of play (60 to 90 minutes of active play on the server), near a random player, with its two llamas. It walks to that player's town when someone is near the town's spawn, or else to a town whose spawn is within 12 chunks of them, and waits by the spawn; with neither, it stays near that player, who alone hears "A travelling trader has arrived near <player>." It stays about 40 minutes.
  • No more invisibility: it never drinks a potion or milk, so you can see it day and night. The town's online members hear "A travelling trader has arrived at <town>."
  • Right-click it, in any town, member or not. It sells for Yen and buys nothing: one Mob Net at ¥10,000, two Saddles at ¥1,000, two Leads at ¥600, two Name Tags at ¥1,500, and three wares that change each visit, sold in bundles (blue ice, packed ice, podzol, moss, coral blocks, cherry, pale oak or mangrove saplings, torchflowers, pitcher plants, cyan dye, pointed dripstone).
  • A click buys one, straight into your bag (never hotbar slot 1), while you stand within 6 m of where it stood when you opened it. The stock is shared: first come, first served. A line turns grey when you cannot pay and dark grey once it is sold out.
  • The vanilla trade window is gone, and the emerald trades with it.
  • A saddle still needs the Stable Master's lesson before you can ride a cow, a sheep or a wolf.
  • A Mob Net nets the trader like any other mob, and it keeps its stock inside the net. Mob Nets now come from Confluence Caches and the travelling trader.
  • /cf trader says what it sells and which town the last one came to.

Towns: outposts

  • A town can now buy one outpost: a 3 x 3 of chunks away from the town, for ¥22,500. The mayor or an assistant stands in the centre chunk of the square and types /cf town outpost buy; the one who buys pays, as with a claim.
  • None of its nine chunks may be claimed already, touch the town (a corner counts), come within 8 chunks of another town or its outpost, or sit too close to the hub or the world spawn. A refusal says why and charges nothing.
  • It does not count toward the town's 64 chunks, and the town cannot grow out from it: a new claim must still touch the town itself.
  • Its chunks are plots like any other: put them up for sale, give them, share them and evict them as on town land. /cf town unclaim will not give them back one at a time.
  • It starts with a copy of the town's four settings and keeps its own after that: the mayor or an assistant uses /cf town outpost mobs off, and the same for trample, crops and stands. /cf town outpost info shows them, and /cf town info names the outpost.
  • On the map the outpost is an area of its own in the town's colour, "<town> outpost", and walking in says "Entering <town> outpost". In edit mode its chunks show as claimed.
  • /cf town outpost release gives the whole 3 x 3 back: nothing is refunded and its plots go with it. The town may then buy another.
  • It has no spawn of its own: walk, ride or set a waypoint. The town spawn stays on the town's own land.

Mob ban

  • In a town with hostile mobs off (/cf town mobs off), endermen and spiders are removed too, like every other hostile mob, and an enderman can no longer teleport in.
  • A mob that attacks you there is removed on sight, even mid-fight. Only your own hit keeps one around, for 8 seconds after your last hit, so a fight you pick is not cut short.
  • A Mob Net will not let an unnamed enderman out in such a town; a named one still comes out.

Chests: the wheel

  • In a chest, barrel, ender chest, shulker box or pack, turn the mouse wheel over a stack to move one item at a time: wheel up moves one into the container, wheel down moves one into your bag.
  • Hover your own stack and wheel down to pull a matching item out of the container; hover the container's stack and wheel up to put a matching one of yours in.
  • The vault window works too, for Breach gear only. Nothing moves in a Breach loot window, a Confluence Cache or a shop menu, and the wheel never touches hotbar slot 1.
  • A bundle keeps its own wheel, and the inventory screen and the crafting grid work as before.

New kit required

  • The mod changed and this update changes the protocol, so an old mod will say "Protocol mismatch".
  • Download the latest zip and, with the game closed, replace confluence-mod-0.1.0.jar in your mods folder with the one from the new zip.
  • The server restarts once for this update.

2026-10-27 Mounts: ride animals, fly a phantom

The Stable Master

  • A new vendor stands in the hub on the training island, by the yard. Right-click him for riding lessons, one per animal, each yours for good: Cow ¥500, Sheep ¥500, Wolf ¥1,000 and Phantom ¥5,000. A lesson you have reads "Learned".
  • Your first lesson comes with a saddle (at your feet if your bag is full).
  • Horses, camels, pigs, striders, happy ghasts and the nautilus still ride as in vanilla, with no lesson.

Riding a cow, a sheep or your wolf

  • Hold a saddle and right-click a grown cow or sheep, or your own tamed, grown wolf, without crouching. The saddle can be in your main hand, or in your off hand while the main hand is empty or holds your Breach weapon, so it works in combat mode with your weapon in slot 1. The saddle is not used up, and a wolf that sat stands up.
  • Not an animal on someone else's lead or tied to a fence, one with a rider, a young one, or the hub's animals. On a town's land you need the town's leave. While you ride, nobody else can put a lead on your animal; if it ends up on someone's lead or tied to a fence anyway, you get off.
  • W, A, S and D move you the way you look, and the animal turns to face it. Hold the sprint key to go about a third faster (a double-tap of W adds nothing). Space jumps, and it steps up a whole block on its own. A line over your hotbar names your keys.
  • The server moves the animal, so it answers your keys a beat late (about your ping plus a tenth to a third of a second). Looking around stays instant.
  • Your head may poke into a low ceiling or through a 2-high door, as on a horse, and it never hurts. If your head goes under water, you get off.
  • Crouch (C in combat mode) or /cf mount off gets you off. The animal stays where you left it and is itself again: your wolf follows you as before.
  • You ride in the main world and the frontier. An incursion's ring and the hub's grounds are closed to riders: you meet them like a wall.

The phantom

  • Once you have its lesson, /cf mount phantom calls it: a phantom swoops in under you and you fly. It needs a little room, and it will not come in a fight, while you fall, while you ride or sit, or within 30 s of sending it away. Each refusal says why.
  • It flies where you look: W to go, look down to dive and up to climb, Space to rise, crouch (C in combat mode) to sink. Let go and it hovers. Your own game steers it, so it answers at once.
  • A crouch in the air never drops you. To get off, land, let go of crouch and press it again: a crouch you held all the way down keeps you on. In water a crouch gets you off.
  • /cf mount off gets you off anywhere. In the air you float down under Slow Falling and take no fall damage on that landing; the Slow Falling ends when you land. On the ground you just step down.
  • It is yours alone: nobody else can climb on, put it on a lead or shear it.
  • It flies in the main world and the frontier only, the Waystation's plaza included: never the Nether, the End, the hub, the Breaches and the Manor, or the Quarry. An incursion's ring, the hub's grounds, a ceiling 16 blocks over the build limit and the edge of the flying area, 4,000 m from the world's spawn, are walls: you slide along them and one line says why.
  • It leaves when you get off, on a teleport, a world change, logging off, a death or a restart, and in the air you float down. Log off in the air (on the phantom, or still floating down from it), or lose the server mid-flight, and you float down when you rejoin.

Fights

  • Mounts are for travel: you cannot get on or call the phantom in a fight, and a fight starting puts you down (off the phantom, you float down).
  • On a walking mount, Evade gets you off without spending a charge, and a cast gets you off and then goes off. On the phantom both wait: "Land first." ("Get off first." once you have landed).
  • A knockback, a pull or a dash now lands in full on any rider: it takes you off a mount, a boat, a minecart or a horse first. Space never double-jumps while you ride.

Your HUD

  • On a mount your hunger bar stays and no mount hearts show. Vanilla horses keep theirs.

Commands

  • /cf mount phantom, /cf mount off and /cf mount list (your lessons and how to ride each). /cf help survival lists them.

New kit required

  • Download the latest zip and replace the confluence-mod jar before you join: the kit draws the phantom and lets you sink with crouch. The server changed too and this update changes the protocol, so an old mod will say "Protocol mismatch".
  • The server restarts once for this update.

2026-10-27 Mount feel test (ops only)

What it is

  • The first step of mounts (riding animals, and a phantom to fly) is a feel test for ops, before anything is built for players. Players see nothing new: no command, no help line.
  • /cf mount test cow, /cf mount test wolf or /cf mount test ghast puts a test mount at your feet with you on it, in the main world or the frontier. You must not be riding anything (a bench counts).
  • The cow and the wolf are moved by the server: W goes the way you look, S backs up, A and D go sideways, the sprint key goes about a third faster, Space jumps, and they step up a whole block. Because the server moves them, they answer your keys a little late (about your ping plus a tenth to a third of a second): that delay is what the test measures.
  • The ghast is a small happy ghast in a black harness, the body the phantom will have. Your own game steers it, as vanilla's happy ghast: W flies the way you look, Space rises. It should answer at once.
  • Crouch to get off: the test mount goes on the next tick. Logging off or a server stop removes it too, so none is left behind. A chat line gives the numbers each test used, and the server log has a [mount] line for each.
  • Land the ghast before you get off. There is no Slow Falling yet, so crouching in the air drops you the whole way, and logging off or a server stop in the air leaves you there to fall when you rejoin.
  • Nobody else can climb onto a test mount or use one: every right-click on one is cancelled.
  • Ride out of other players' way and away from any Nether or End portal: a test mount is not stopped at a portal yet.

Tuning without a restart

  • The speeds, the sprint bonus, the jump, the step height and the ghast's size and flying speed are in mounts.json. Copy the bundled file (plugin/src/main/resources/confluence-content/mounts.json in the repo) to plugins/Confluence/content/mounts.json on the server, change a number, get off and run /cf mount test again: it reads the file afresh every time.
  • A bad number or a misspelt key is refused with a line naming it, and nothing spawns. A broken copy left there at a restart does not stop the plugin: the log names it and the test refuses until it is fixed.
  • Config mounts.enabled: false (then a restart) turns the test off.

Testing at a friend's ping

  • Test from a second PC over the network, not on the host (the host's own ping is near zero and hides the delay).
  • Use a network shaper on that PC, as the boss checks do ("set ping to 240 ms with a network shaper"), and run each mount at 0, 120 and 240 ms of ping. A shaper that delays both directions adds its delay twice: 60 ms each way is a 120 ms ping, 120 ms each way a 240 ms ping.
  • The course and the notes to take are the MOUNT FEEL checks in docs/TESTING.md (MF1 to MF5); Bibidi's verdict and the chosen numbers decide the next step.

No new kit needed

  • This update is all on the server (one restart): your current jar works, and the Discord bot is unchanged.

2026-10-27 Benches, teleport requests, Mob Nets and Confluence Caches

Benches

  • Sit on any stair or slab, like a bench. Crouch, right-click a stair that is the right way up (its step at the bottom) or a slab in the lower half of its block, then let go of crouch. You sit facing out of the stair's open side; on a slab you face the way you were looking.
  • Your hands must be empty. In combat mode your Breach weapon may stay in hand, and crouch is your crouch key (C unless you changed it).
  • Stand up with crouch or jump (the line over your hotbar names your own keys). In combat mode Evade stands you up without spending a charge, and an ability stands you up and then goes off.
  • You stand back where you were when you sat down, or just above it if a block is in the way now; never above lava, fire or powder snow (then on the step itself).
  • Not in a fight: a fight that starts stands you up, and so does any knockback, pull or teleport, which then lands in full. A seat never shields you.
  • One person a seat. Upside-down stairs, top slabs and double slabs are not seats, and neither is a stair with no room above it.
  • Every world, the hub's dais and the Waystation's shelter benches included. Sitting changes no block, so you can sit on another player's plot.
  • Outside a fight, a crouch-click with empty hands on a free stair or slab seats you instead of starting Berserk.

Teleport requests

  • /tpa <name> asks to teleport to a player; /tpahere <name> asks them to teleport to you. They get one chat line with a green [Accept] and a red [Deny] (hover either to see what it does), and a notice on their HUD.
  • On Accept the one who moves goes at once, no hold, and lands beside the other player: on solid ground, never in lava or a block, and never in a gate, a Quarry pad or a portal (nothing fires as you arrive). Joining someone sitting on a bench puts you where they would stand up.
  • /tpaccept and /tpdeny answer by typing; with several requests waiting they list them, so add the name (/tpaccept Bob). An old line's buttons never answer a newer request.
  • A request lapses after 60 s. You have one out at a time: a new one withdraws your last. After a Deny you wait 60 s before asking that player again.
  • Where: the survival worlds, their Nether and the hub; the End only with a Wilds Pass (yours or a party member's), the Quarry only with Quarry time of your own (a request never spends a ticket). Never into or out of a Breach, and not while either of you is in a fight.
  • /tptoggle turns requests to you off (or /tptoggle off and /tptoggle on): nobody can ask, party and town included. It stays that way across logins and restarts.
  • Ops: /cf tphere <name> or /cf tphere all brings players at once (players in a Breach run are skipped and named).
  • /cf help teleport lists the commands.

Mob Nets

  • A Mob Net catches a mob whole so you can let it out somewhere else. Hold an empty net and right-click a mob: it goes in with everything it has, its saddle and armour, a horse's colour and markings, a donkey's or llama's chest and what is in it, its name, its health, a villager's trades, job and level, and a pet's owner. The net turns into that mob's egg with a glint and its lore says what it holds.
  • Right-click a block with a filled net and the mob comes out on that side, facing you, as it was. A mob you let out stays: it never despawns when you walk away. A chest, a door or anything else you can click keeps its click unless you sneak.
  • Nets are unstable: each release has a 1 in 4 chance to break the net. The roll comes after the mob is out, so you never lose a mob to a break.
  • A dispenser fired with a filled net lets the mob out in front of it and keeps the emptied net (unless it broke). A locked dispenser fires nothing, and a dropper just drops the net.
  • Where: the survival worlds and their Nether and End, only where you may build (at both ends), and never on the spots the gates land players. Not in a fight, one mob a net, and a second between uses. A town with hostile mobs off turns away an unnamed hostile mob ("Oakvale keeps hostile mobs out: the Zombie would vanish there.").
  • What slips the net: bosses, Breach and other Confluence enemies, the Wither, wardens, elder guardians, shulkers, someone else's pet, a mob being ridden, riding or on a lead, a mob hunting a player or just out of a fight with one, a villager mid-trade and a trial spawner's mob. An idle hostile mob is fair game. A click on a player, a vendor or the hub's animals works as ever.
  • Nets come from Confluence Caches (below); /cf net says how they work and how many filled and empty nets you carry. /cf trade refuses a net, so drop it for a friend. A mob from a spawner or an egg still pays no profession XP after a trip in a net.
  • Ops: /cf net give <name> [n] hands out 1 to 16 empty nets.

Confluence Caches

  • While you play in the overworld wilds (the main world or the frontier), a Confluence Cache lands 40 to 120 m from you every 45 to 90 minutes of active play. Time AFK (five minutes idle), in the hub, a Breach, the Quarry, the Nether or the End, or while your last cache still stands does not count; a fight holds a due cache back until it ends.
  • Chat and your HUD say where: "A Confluence Cache landed 80 m north-east of you." Anyone else within 160 m hears it too, with their own distance and direction, and gets a pennant on the map (M), "Confluence Cache (the Seam)": right-click it and choose Guide here for the arrow, the distance and the beam. Nobody else is told, and nothing is posted in Discord.
  • A streak of light falls, a muffled boom, and a small crater of end stone and coarse dirt appears with a chest sunk at its centre, a floating label with the minutes left and a column of smoke you can follow from 160 m.
  • One cache for whoever reaches it, first come, first served: everyone sees the same 27 slots and the first click wins. You can take things out (click, shift-click, the drop key, a number key onto an empty slot) but nothing goes in. Always a Mob Net, sometimes two; often a purse of Yen (take it and the Yen go straight to your wallet); and two to four more: experience bottles, leads, a name tag, a saddle, an enchanted book, iron, copper or gold ingots, redstone, lapis, an ender pearl or two, arrows, now and then a Quarry Ticket or a market find. About ¥4,000 of value and 1.25 Mob Nets a cache, and about 0.7 to 0.9 caches an hour of play.
  • Until it fades nobody can break or build in the crater, pour into it (by hand or with a dispenser), blow it up or push it with a piston, set a boat, minecart, armour stand, end crystal, item frame or painting down in it, or reach the chest with a hopper, a second chest or a copper golem. A cache cannot be locked or titled, and a filled Mob Net clicked on it just opens it.
  • It fades 20 minutes after it lands, or 30 seconds once emptied: the chest, the label and the pennant go, the ground comes back exactly as it was, and anyone or anything in the bowl is lifted onto the ground (a player who logged out there at their next join). What is left inside is lost. A restart fades every cache.
  • Never in or beside a town, by a /home or a bed, on or by the Waystation and its trails, the hub or the world spawn, by an incursion or another cache, on or beside a build, a village or a farm, in water or lava, under trees, on uneven ground or within 8 m of anyone.
  • Ops: /cf cache now [player], /cf cache here, /cf cache list and /cf cache clear [id]. The numbers, the sites, the crater and the loot table are in the new content file caches.json (copy it to plugins/Confluence/content/ to change them), and config caches.enabled: false stops new landings.

No new kit needed

  • This update is all on the server (one restart): your current jar works (the caches' map pennant rides the waypoint list it already draws), and the Discord bot is unchanged.
  • Before that restart Bibidi sets spawn-protection=0 in the server's server.properties, so everyone can sit on the Waystation's benches (the Waystation's grounds stay protected by the server's own spawn zone).

2026-10-26 Target outlines
