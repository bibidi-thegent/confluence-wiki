Settlements

For sale: a Settler founds towns to sell. A listed one is marked "(for sale: ¥5,000)" on the map and has a Buy line at the Town Warden. In no town? Stand in it and type /cf town buy to see its price, then /cf town buy <price> (/cf town buy 5000), or click Buy at the Warden: the price goes to the Settler and you become its mayor, with every mayor power. If the price changed meanwhile, nothing is taken.
